Finding your quiet place…

A field guide to motes

A place to be. A world to discover.

Choose neon rain or golden hour, press Listen, and settle in. Click the water to send a ripple. The moving lights in the pond are living cells; their positions, energy and pulses come from the simulation. Rain and meadow breeze add a quiet sound bed. Hide controls to leave just the scene.

Explore reveals the communities within the same painted water. Select a light to follow its colony, or reveal energy and connections. These lenses also trace the submerged shelves and food sources of the model. The scenery is an artistic setting; switching lenses keeps your world running.

Each point of light is a cell. Some gather nourishment, some propel the body, and some hold it together. Their bonds carry energy and coordinate a shared pulse. What you hear is that coordination, translated into sound.

Observe
Select a creature to see its cells and rhythm. Drag the water to look around. Scroll or pinch to move closer.
Nourish
Place a finite food source. Watch which cells find it, and where their energy goes.
Current
Send a local swirl through the pool. Strong currents stretch connections and can split a body.
Sever
Cut the connections beneath your cursor. Fragments must survive with the cells they have.
Branch
Rewind, then branch to preserve the original outcome. Change something and compare both versions at the same moment.

The pond and crossing begin with prepared colonies. Scattered beginnings starts with unconnected cells. Their subsequent motion, feeding, bonding, growth and death follow the same local rules. There is no scheduled ending.
