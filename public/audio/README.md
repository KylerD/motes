# Piano recordings

The locally bundled piano notes are from **Upright Piano KW (small), 2019-07-03**, recorded by **Gonzalo and Roberto** in January 2017 and edited by Roberto for the FreePats project. The instrument is a Kawai upright recorded in a living room. Thank you to Inma Martínez de Miguel for access to the piano.

- Primary source and provenance: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html#UprightKW
- Original archive: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/UprightPianoKW/UprightPianoKW-small-SFZ+FLAC-20190703.7z
- License: **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication**. https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original source note: [piano/SOURCE.txt](piano/SOURCE.txt)
- Full dedication: [piano/CC0.txt](piano/CC0.txt)

Motes selects twelve original recordings (B2, D#3, F#3, A3, C4, D#4, F#4, A4, C5, D#5, F#5, A5), names them by MIDI note, limits them to five seconds with a final 400ms fade where applicable, resamples to 32kHz, and encodes MP3 using FFmpeg/libmp3lame quality 4. It pitch-shifts nearby notes, applies a low-pass filter, and adds a short room reverb at playback. No remote audio service is used at runtime.

Bass, drums, ambience, composition, and arrangement are generated by the Motes music engine. There are no third-party song recordings or loops.
